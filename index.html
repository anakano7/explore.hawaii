Welcome to Explore Hawaiʻi

Hawaiʻi has many landscapes to explore, from beaches and volcanic mountains to coastal towns and green valleys. This website introduces places on four islands: Oʻahu, Maui, Kauaʻi, and Hawaiʻi Island.

Choose an Island

Each island page introduces three places with descriptions and a photograph. Choose an island below to learn more.

  • Oʻahu — Explore Waikīkī, Diamond Head, and the North Shore.
  • Maui — Learn about Haleakalā, Hāna, and Kāʻanapali.
  • Kauaʻi — Discover Waimea Canyon, the Nāpali Coast, and Poʻipū.
  • Hawaiʻi Island — Explore Hawaiʻi Volcanoes National Park, Hilo, and Kailua-Kona.

Respect the Places You Explore

These places are part of living communities. Respect private property, follow posted rules, and leave natural and historic places as you find them. Learning about local history and Hawaiian place names can help deepen your understanding of the islands.
