Explore Oʻahu

This page introduces places to explore on Oʻahu. Each place offers a different way to experience the island, from the coast to historic landmarks.

Places to Explore

  • Waikīkī
  • Diamond Head
  • North Shore

Waikīkī

Waikīkī is a neighborhood in Honolulu known for its beach and ocean views. I chose it for this website because it is one of the places I want to highlight on Oʻahu.

Diamond Head

Diamond Head, also called Lēʻahi, is a volcanic crater on Oʻahu. Its distinctive shape makes it a recognizable part of the island’s landscape.

North Shore

Oʻahu’s North Shore is known for its beaches and surfing. It offers a different setting from the busy streets of Waikīkī.
